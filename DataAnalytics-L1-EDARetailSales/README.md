# OIBSIP - EDA on Retail Sales Data

## Objective
To analyse retail sales data and identify sales trends, customer behaviour, and product category performance.

## Tools Used
- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Google Colab

## Dataset
Retail Sales Dataset containing 1,000 transactions and 9 original columns.

## Analysis Performed
- Dataset inspection and statistical summary
- Missing values and duplicate checking
- Monthly and quarterly sales trends
- Customer gender and age group analysis
- Revenue and quantity sold by product category
- Correlation heatmap
- Gender-wise sales contribution

## Conclusion
The analysis helps identify retail sales patterns and provides insights for inventory planning, marketing, and business decisions.
